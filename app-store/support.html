Support

Forest Pomodoro: focus, grow, breathe.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email asametdurann@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. For bugs, please include your device, operating system and version, the app version (for example 1.0.0), and the steps that led to the problem.

Frequently asked questions

How does it work?

Pick a tag and a session length, then tap Plant a tree. A seed grows into a full tree while the timer runs. When the session ends, the tree joins your forest and you earn one coin per focused minute. Take a short break, and after a few sessions a long one.

Why did my tree wither?

A tree withers if you tap Give up, or, with Wither when I leave turned on, if you leave the app or browser tab for longer than the grace period. You can change both in Settings. Withered trees stay in your forest so you can see your focus honestly.

How do coins and new trees work?

You earn 1 coin per minute of every completed session. Spend coins in the Seeds tab to unlock Quiet Pine, Blossom Cherry and Ember Maple. Coins have no monetary value and can't be bought.

Do I need an account? Does it work offline?

No account is needed and the app works offline. Your forest is saved on your device. Create an optional account in Settings to back it up and sync between devices; trees you grew as a guest are kept.

I forgot my password

Open Settings → Account → Forgot password? and enter your email. Open the link we send you on the same device, then choose a new password.

How do I delete my data or account?

Without an account, uninstall the app (or clear the website's data) to remove everything. With an account, email asametdurann@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we'll delete your data within 30 days.

Install on Android (free APK)

Open the download link for the .apk on your phone, allow installing from your browser when Android asks, and open the file. Updates are installed the same way.

Install on iPhone or any phone (web app)

Open the website in Safari on iPhone and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen). It then opens full-screen like a normal app and works offline.

The timer kept running in the background, is that right?

Yes. The timer is based on the clock, so it stays accurate when you lock the phone or reload the page. Alerts can only play while the app is open.
