Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026

In short: Forest Pomodoro works without an account, and then nothing leaves your device. If you choose to create an account, we store your email address and your focus history so we can back it up and sync it between your devices. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Forest Pomodoro ("the app") is developed by Abdulsamet Duran, who is responsible for your data (the data controller). Contact: asametdurann@gmail.com.

Using the app without an account

Your focus sessions, trees, coins, unlocked species and settings are stored only on your device (or in your browser's storage when you use the web version). We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the browser's site data, deletes it.

Data we collect if you create an account

DataWhy
Email addressTo create and secure your account, sign you in and send account emails (confirmation, password reset).
PasswordTo sign you in. It is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we never see it.
Focus sessions (start and end time, planned and focused duration, tag, tree species, result, coins earned)To back up your forest and statistics and sync them across your devices.
Profile (coin balance, unlocked and selected species, focus streak, timer and alert settings)To keep your progress and preferences in sync.
Account identifier and technical data (such as IP address and request logs kept by our providers)To operate the service, keep it secure and prevent abuse.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, health data, advertising identifiers or analytics. The app plays a sound and can vibrate when a timer ends, and can keep the screen awake during a session; none of this collects data.

Legal bases (EU/UK users)

We process account data to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract) and to keep it secure (legitimate interests). You can stop using the account features at any time.

Service providers

  • Supabase: account authentication and database hosting (AWS, London region eu-west-2). Your data is protected so that each account can only read its own records.
  • Vercel: hosts the web version of the app; it keeps standard web server logs.
  • Expo / EAS: builds the mobile app; it does not receive your app data.

These providers process data only on our behalf. Some may process data outside your country; they use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On iPhone and Android, sign-in tokens are kept in the system's secure storage (Keychain / Keystore). Database access rules ensure that each account can only read and change its own data.

How long we keep data

Account data is kept while your account exists. When you sign out, the app removes its copy from that device. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your Forest Pomodoro data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its processing, by emailing asametdurann@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, but accounts are not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and revise the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
